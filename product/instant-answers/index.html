Top 10 products by revenue in the past 30 days

By Rob D Added Jan 02, 2026
Published

Question
What are the top 10 products by revenue in the last 30 days?
Auto Description

This answer identifies your best-selling products by revenue contribution over the last 30 days.

"Revenue" is defined as the sum of product_price from the order_items table.
"Last 30 days" is interpreted as the 30-day period immediately preceding the current timestamp when the query is executed.

This answer:

  • Looks at all order line items placed in the past 30 days.
  • Groups those sales by product.
  • Calculates total revenue per product as the sum of product_price across all qualifying order items.
  • Ranks products from highest to lowest revenue.
  • Returns the top 10 revenue ranks, including ties (so if multiple products share the same revenue as the 10th-ranked product, they will all be included).
