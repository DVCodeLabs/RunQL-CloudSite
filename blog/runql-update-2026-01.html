Product Update: Workspace 2.0

runQL Team • January 2026

Starting 2026 strong with major upgrades to workspaces, request management, and the AI models powering runQL.

Jan 2026 Shipping Themes

  • Workspace & request management
  • Answer viewing
  • Model upgrades

Workspace & Request Management

Better tools for organizing your analytics work:

  • Workspace Management: Comprehensive improvements to how you manage workspaces.
  • Request UX: Right-sidebar request tab, improved default focus, and a cleaner inbox layout.

Org Controls & Platform

  • LLM Upgrade: We've upgraded from GPT-4o to GPT-5.2 (and Mini to GPT-5-Mini) for smarter, faster answers.
  • Instant Answers: New organization settings for managing Instant Answers and Request Management, including faster "review all" workflows.
  • Answer Cards: Cards can now open results in a modal for quick viewing.

  • Connection fixes for non-admins
